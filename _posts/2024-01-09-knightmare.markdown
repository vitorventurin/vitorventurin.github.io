**Role:** Sole developer: game design, programming and music.

### Highlights

- Four levels, each with its own environment and challenges.
- Light and dark variants of the weapons, which adapt to the level.
- Boss battle with an animated boss and multiple attack phases.
- Collectibles: coins, health potions and speed potions.
- Soundtrack mixing the original MSX music with my own remixes.
- Keyboard and gamepad support.

### Tech

Godot 4, GDScript, custom shaders, signal-driven architecture, 2D physics layers
