**Role:** Sole developer of the iOS, Android and web versions.

### Highlights

- Built the native iOS app in Swift with MVVM and the Coordinator pattern, using protocols for dependency injection and testability.
- Recipes live in Firebase Firestore with offline persistence, so the app keeps working without a connection.
- Text-to-speech narration of recipes with a choice of Brazilian or European Portuguese voice.
- Pinterest-style photo grid, parallax header on the recipe screen, and recipe sharing.
- Refreshed the app in 2025 (version 1.1): safer concurrency around Firestore setup, fewer third-party dependencies, updated unit tests.

### Android and web

- Built the Android version in React Native with TypeScript: paginated recipe list, masonry grid, debounced search over title and ingredients, light and dark mode, Jest tests.
- Built a Progressive Web App in React: masonry grid with infinite scroll, offline support through a Workbox service worker, automatic dark mode, hosted on Firebase.

### Tech

Swift, UIKit, MVVM, Coordinator, AVFoundation, Kingfisher, Firebase (Firestore, Storage), React Native, TypeScript, React, Workbox, Jest
