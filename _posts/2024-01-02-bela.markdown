**Role:** Sole developer.

### Highlights

- Single-page React app with no backend: it runs entirely in the browser and is deployed as a static site on GitHub Pages.
- Photos auto-advance every 10 seconds and are centered on screen whatever their orientation, portrait or landscape.
- Started with Unsplash as the photo source, then moved to the Google Photos Picker so people can use their own photos.
- Picked photos are downloaded once and cached in IndexedDB, so the screensaver keeps working after a reload without signing in or picking again.
- Google sign-in is handled with Google Identity Services and silent token renewal, with no server-side secret.

### Tech

React, JavaScript, Google Photos Picker API, Google Identity Services, IndexedDB, GitHub Pages
