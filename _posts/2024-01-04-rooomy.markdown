**Role:** iOS developer in a small team that built the app from scratch.

### Highlights

- Augmented Reality furniture and décor placement built on ARKit.
- Universal app, supporting both iPhone and iPad.
- Structured with the VIPER architecture.
- Worked with CI/CD, code reviews and unit testing as part of the team workflow.

### Tech

ARKit, VIPER, ReplayKit, CoreData, CI/CD, unit testing
